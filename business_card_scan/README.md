# Business Card Scanner

Take a photo of a business card and create a CRM lead, with free in-browser OCR (Tesseract.js) and no IAP credits.

- Menu: **Business Cards → Scan** / **Business Cards → Cards**
- Dependencies: `crm`
- Parser tests: `python3 business_card_scan/tests/test_card_parser.py -v`

Full documentation: see the [repository README](../README.md).
